# MITRA business roadmap: $0 to a $1B company

This is a strategy framework, not a forecast or guarantee. A $1B valuation, $1B annual recurring revenue and $1B cumulative sales are three different outcomes; the company must state which one it is targeting and report actual audited metrics.

## Stage 0 — $0 to first 10 design partners

**Goal:** prove a painful workflow is solved better than existing MCP servers and automation platforms.

- Ship a stable, easy-to-clone open-source core.
- Interview 30-50 developers and small teams.
- Pick one initial wedge: secure, per-agent integration and task governance.
- Recruit 10 design partners; measure setup time, successful task completion, errors and retention.
- Keep hosting optional. Earn trust through transparent code, docs and security practices.

**Exit criteria:** repeat usage, measurable time saved, low setup failure rate, and at least 3 customers willing to pay for support or integrations.

## Stage 1 — $10K to $100K annualized revenue

- Paid support, onboarding, adapter development and team configuration.
- Publish tested adapters and clear capability manifests.
- Introduce signed releases, upgrade/rollback and compatibility tests.
- Build a public roadmap and community contribution process.

**Metrics:** paid conversion, gross margin, support hours per customer, 90-day retention.

## Stage 2 — $100K to $1M ARR

- Team edition: shared policies, centrally managed adapter catalog and audit export.
- Marketplace revenue share for third-party capabilities.
- Paid enterprise connectors and compliance documentation.
- Add usage analytics only with clear opt-in and privacy controls.

**Metrics:** ARR, net revenue retention, customer acquisition cost, payback period, churn.

## Stage 3 — $1M to $10M ARR

- Enterprise security: SSO, role-based access, key management, policy-as-code and audit evidence.
- Partner program for agencies, model providers and system integrators.
- Support multiple execution environments without forcing a single cloud.
- Establish customer success, security response and release engineering.

## Stage 4 — $10M to $100M ARR

- Global sales and support, regional partners and localization.
- Marketplace governance, certification and developer revenue tools.
- Independent security audits, disaster recovery and reliability SLOs.
- Expand into business workflows only where customer demand and unit economics are proven.

## Stage 5 — $100M to $1B scale

- Multiple product lines and business units with accountable executives.
- Strategic acquisitions only when integration and return are measurable.
- Board-level risk, finance, security, privacy and compliance oversight.
- Audited financial statements, internal controls and succession planning.
- Keep human executives accountable for legal, financial, hiring and safety-critical decisions.

## After crossing a $1B milestone

The system should help management operate the company, not become an unaccountable CEO. MITRA can prepare forecasts, monitor budgets, surface risks, draft plans, coordinate approved workflows and produce board reporting. Human officers and the board retain authority over corporate actions.

## Revenue model hypotheses

1. Open-source core: free and self-hostable.
2. Team/enterprise control plane: per-seat or per-instance subscription.
3. Certified integrations: paid adapter licenses or marketplace commission.
4. Enterprise support and implementation.
5. Optional hosted relay/control plane for customers who want it — never a requirement for the core.

## Non-negotiable gates

- Do not treat valuation as revenue.
- Do not automate spam, deceptive outreach or unauthorized account access.
- Never spend customer money or execute financial transfers without explicit delegated authority and transaction-level controls.
- Do not claim an adapter or autonomous feature works until it has integration tests and a published support matrix.
